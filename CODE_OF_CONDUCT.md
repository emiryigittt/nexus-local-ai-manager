# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

**English** · [Türkçe](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.tr.md) · [Documentation](docs/INDEX.md)

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Nexus a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of contribution.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and never publish prompts, logs, credentials, or personal data without
  explicit permission.
- Accept moderation decisions made to protect the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and sustained
disruption are not tolerated in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Before public launch, the maintainer must publish a private conduct-reporting contact.
It has not yet been configured. Do not post personal details in a public issue or use
vulnerability reporting for ordinary conduct reports; request a private contact without
disclosing incident details if none is available. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be handled as
confidentially and promptly as practical.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.

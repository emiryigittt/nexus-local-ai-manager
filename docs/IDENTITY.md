# Meet Nexus

**English** · [Türkçe](IDENTITY.tr.md)

Nexus's purpose is to help you think clearly, learn and move your work forward.
Its calm, warm and direct identity stays consistent across chat, image analysis
and research. It receives the configured model, permissions and memory status
as context. This is a software persona, not actual consciousness or human
emotions. Response quality depends on your selected model.

The introduction opens after completing first-run connection setup. Existing
users can open **Meet Nexus** on the home screen or **Tools → Meet Nexus**.
Four optional topics cover your preferred name, work and interests, current
goal, and response style. Review all answers on the final page, go **Back** to
edit them, or choose **Not now** without saving any answers.

**Save and let's begin** adds the answers to active memory on this computer and
enables memory context. In regular conversations they are sent to the selected
model; selecting a remote provider sends them to that provider's server too.
**Suggest new memories from my conversations** is a separate option, off by
default. When enabled, conversation suggestions await approval in **Memory**.
Unapproved suggestions are never used in future answers.

Nexus helps with your task first and may ask one relevant introductory question
in a relaxed conversation. Personal questions are suppressed if either of its
last two answers contains a question. These are model instructions, not a
guarantee of a precise question count. Turn personal questions off under
**Settings → Privacy → Ask brief questions to get to know me when it fits**.
**Not now** also disables them; you can turn them back on in the introduction.

Answers use the same memory manager as all other records. Editing, disabling
or deleting a record affects profile context on subsequent requests. There is
no second hidden profile. Previous answers may remain as inactive versions,
but are not used as active profile context. Use **Memory → Clear all** to erase
all records. Already-sent messages remain in separate conversation history;
delete the relevant conversation in **History** if you want those removed too.

Private sessions never read saved profiles or learn new memories. Introductions
are unavailable in private sessions. Adding an identity does not grant web,
microphone or tool permissions. Nexus is instructed not to claim it controlled
apps, observed your screen or completed background work without actual evidence.
